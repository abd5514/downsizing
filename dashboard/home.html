Driver drowsiness detection

Wakes a tired driver without sending their face anywhere.

DrowSim watches the driver through an ordinary camera, spots eye closure and yawning on the device itself, and sounds the alarm locally. Only a handful of numbers per frame ever leave the vehicle.

How it works

Two tiers, one set of rules.

1 · In the vehicle

Detect on the device

A YOLOv8n model reads each camera frame and reports how alert, sleepy or yawning the driver looks. The frame is discarded straight away.

2 · Still in the vehicle

Decide and alarm locally

Eyes closed for 0.8 seconds raise a critical alarm at once, with no internet needed. Eye closure over the last minute and repeated yawning raise warnings.

3 · On this server

Monitor the fleet

The numbers reach this server, where a BiLSTM model adds a second opinion and the dashboard shows every vehicle's state as it changes.

What it detects

The detector sorts every frame into one of three driver states.

ALERT

Eyes open, attentive

Shown as NORMAL. Ordinary blinks under 0.4 seconds are ignored.

YAWNING

An early sign of fatigue

Shown as WARNING, and three yawns within five minutes raise a warning on their own.

MICROSLEEP

Eyes closed too long

Shown as CRITICAL the moment the eyes stay closed for 0.8 seconds.

Privacy by design

What crosses the network is seven numbers per frame.

No video leaves the car

Frames live in one variable on the device and are overwritten by the next one.

Nothing is stored

This server keeps sessions in memory only. Its history is gone when it restarts.

Works offline

The alarm runs on the device, so a lost connection never silences it.
